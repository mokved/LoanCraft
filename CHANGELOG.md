# Changelog

## 1.1.0

First public release, for WoW: Forever.

- **Just trade or mail gold:** when gold changes hands, LoanCraft asks what it was (new loan with terms, repayment, lend more, or not a loan). If both players run LoanCraft, one answer settles it for both.
- **Pay and Lend buttons** next to the trade window and mailbox fill in the gold and record it without asking.
- **Player picker:** suggests names as you type, from target, loan partners, recent trades/mail/whispers, group, friends and guild. Shift-click a name in chat to fill it in.
- **Coin boxes** for gold, silver and copper.
- **Interest, due dates and late fees,** with defaults in Options. The terms you last gave each player are remembered.
- **Ledger tab:** every transaction with interest split out, a profit/loss summary (all time, 30 days, 7 days) and CSV export.
- **People tab** with a reliability rating per player.
- **Minimap button** with a red, orange or blue status ring and a count badge.
- **Debts on player tooltips;** login, due-soon and overdue reminders.
- **Sync** with the other player's LoanCraft. The lender stays in charge of the record.
