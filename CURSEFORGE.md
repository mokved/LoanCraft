# LoanCraft

Lend gold to friends and guildies without a spreadsheet. LoanCraft keeps the books for you.

> ⚠️ **Important: LoanCraft cannot make anyone pay you back.** It only keeps records. It can't take gold, lock items or enforce a loan in any way. Once you hand gold over, getting it back depends entirely on the other player. **Use LoanCraft only with players you trust, and for roleplay purposes.** Never lend gold you can't afford to lose.

## How it works
Just **trade or mail gold**. LoanCraft notices and asks what it was:
- a **new loan**, with interest, due date and late fee
- a **repayment** of an existing loan
- **lending more** on top of a loan
- or **not a loan** at all

If the other player also runs LoanCraft, the loan shows up on their side to accept, and **one answer settles it for both of you**.

## Features
- **Pay / Lend buttons** next to the trade window and mailbox fill in the exact amount.
- **Player picker** with suggestions from your target, loan partners, recent trades/mail/whispers, group, friends and guild. You can also shift-click a name in chat.
- **Separate gold / silver / copper boxes.**
- **Interest** per day, week or month, **due dates** and **late fees**. LoanCraft remembers the terms you gave each player.
- **Ledger** of every loan and repayment, with interest split out, a **profit/loss summary** and CSV export.
- **People** tab with a **reliability rating** ("3 of 4 repaid on time").
- **Minimap button** that glows **red** when something is overdue, **orange** when it's due soon, and **blue** when something needs your decision.
- **Player tooltips** show "owes you 50g".
- **Reminders** at login, before due dates and when overdue.

## Fair play
The lender is in charge of the record, so nobody can erase their own debt. A debt someone says you owe needs your OK. LoanCraft only records gold that actually changed hands. It can't take gold or force anyone to repay, so use it only with players you trust and for roleplay purposes.

## Getting around
Everything is in the UI, with no chat commands. Click the **gold coin on your minimap** to open LoanCraft, shift-click it for a new loan, or right-click it for the menu.
